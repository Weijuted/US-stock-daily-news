# Market Events and Macro Factors – October 13, 2026

## Primary Market Catalysts

### 1. Q3 Earnings Season Kickoff – Big Banks
The day was dominated by the first wave of major bank earnings for Q3 2026.

**Goldman Sachs (GS):**
- Beat estimates decisively; EPS ~$17.01 vs consensus ~$12.90–13.02
- Equities trading revenue jumped 72% to $6.44 billion
- Stock faded after initial rally, ended -0.23%
- Source: 247WallSt, CNBC bank earnings

**JPMorgan Chase (JPM):**
- Missed expectations: EPS ~$5.57 vs estimate ~$5.90
- Still raised 2026 net interest income guidance
- Stock ended -0.9%
- Pre-market had been down ~3.5% before recovering
- Source: QZ.com, CNBC

**Wells Fargo (WFC):**
- Mixed Q3 results; stock fell 3%
- Source: CNBC, 247WallSt

### 2. Memory Chip Selloff
- China's CXMT announced plans to release a more powerful memory chip by year-end
- South Korea KOSPI tumbled 5.5% to weakest close since July 31
- MSCI Emerging Markets Index fell ~2.2%
- US memory chip names hit hard (see individual stocks section)
- Semiconductor weakness weighed on Nasdaq

## Macro Factors

### Federal Reserve / Monetary Policy
- Current policy rate: 3.75–4.00% (raised at September FOMC meeting, 12-0 unanimous vote)
- Effective federal funds rate: 3.88% (H.15 data through Oct 8)
- Next FOMC decisions: October 27–28, then December 9
- Fed officials expect another hike before year-end (per September minutes, released Oct 7)
- Markets price 3 more hikes through mid-2027, including 1 more in 2026
- Source: CNBC (Fed minutes): https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/07/fed-officials-see-another-hike-coming-but-no-sign-as-to-when-minutes-show.html

### Inflation / CPI
- Core PCE: 3.3% as of July 2026 (up from 3.0% in Dec 2025)
- Cleveland Fed nowcast (Oct 13): Headline CPI ~3.63% YoY; Core CPI ~2.30%; Core PCE ~3.10%
- CRITICAL: September 2026 CPI report due October 14 (next morning, 8:30 ET)
  - Prior headline CPI: 3.4% YoY, 0.4% MoM
  - Forecasts: ~3.6–3.7% headline; ~2.4% core
  - High market anticipation going into October 13 session
- Source: https://admiralmarkets.com/analytics/traders-blog/us-cpi-preview-september-2026-forecasts-and-the-core-cpi-outlook

### Treasury Yields
- 10-year Treasury yield: ~5.22–5.24% (near 24-year highs per some sources)
- 2-year Treasury yield: ~4.75% (Fed H.15 data)
- Rising yield environment continues to weigh on growth stocks and rate-sensitive sectors
- Yield-sensitive Blackstone fell 4.18% on rising yields + analyst price target cuts

### Oil Prices
- Oil rose approximately 1.6% on the session on Middle East supply concerns
- Ongoing geopolitical tensions in Middle East (US-Iran dynamics, supply disruption fears)
- Source: Multiple market recaps

### Gold
- Gold fell 0.21% to $4,181.80

## Overall Market Tone
- Near-flat day; all major indexes finished in narrow range
- Selling concentrated in: memory/AI chips, rate-sensitive sectors (Blackstone), healthcare (J&J)
- Offsetting support from bank earnings (Goldman beat) and energy
- Market focus pivoting to next day's CPI release
